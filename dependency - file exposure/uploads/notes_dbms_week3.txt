DBMS Week 3 Notes — Normalisation
==================================
Uploaded by: rohan.mehta@rvce.edu.in

1NF: No repeating groups. Each column must have atomic values.
2NF: 1NF + no partial dependency on a composite key.
3NF: 2NF + no transitive dependencies.
BCNF: Stricter form of 3NF — every determinant must be a candidate key.

Example:
  Student(rollNo, name, courseId, courseName, marks)
  Problem: courseName depends on courseId, not rollNo.
  Fix:     Split into Student(rollNo, name) and Course(courseId, courseName)
            and Enrolment(rollNo, courseId, marks).
